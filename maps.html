Amsterdam — into your Google Maps

Two ways to get all 70+ pins onto your phone. The first is the real thing; the second works straight away with no account.

1. The proper annotated map (Google My Maps) — 4 taps

Why you have to do this bit: a My Map lives inside a Google account, so it can't be created for you from outside — that's the bit that blocked us last time.
  1. Open mymaps.google.com on a laptop or phone, signed in.
  2. Create a new map → Import (under the first layer) → Upload → choose amsterdam-places.kml — download it here: amsterdam-places.kml
  3. It arrives as layers named by category (Your hotel, Restaurants, Bars, Museums…); if Maps flattens them into one, each pin still keeps its own icon and its notes. Nothing to place by hand.
  4. Share → change "Private" to "Anyone with the link" → copy the link into the group chat.
On a phone, open that share link with Google Maps installed — it opens in the Maps app, and the map appears later under Saved → Maps so you don't need the link again.

2. Routes that open in the Maps app right now

No account needed, and each link is shareable in WhatsApp. Tap on the phone and it opens the Google Maps app with every stop named on the map and in the list.
Google caps a single route at nine stops, so a city's-worth of pins has to be split — that's what these are.
