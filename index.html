Arriving from Schiphol

Gate to hotel in five steps, with bags, on a wet Friday.

  1. Land and clear

    Passport control and bags.

    30-45 min

  2. Train under the terminal

    Buy or tap in. Paper adds €1.60, so just tap a bank card.

    5 min, €5.50

  3. Sprinter to Centraal

    Normally platform 3, eight trains an hour.

    16-17 min

  4. Out the IJ side, ferry F2

    Walk straight on. No ticket, no barrier.

    4-5 min

  5. IJplein to Motorkade 1

    Flat, along the waterside.

    9 min, 552 m

Gate to hotel runs 45 to 55 minutes. With seven of you and bags, call it an hour. In rain, take bus 34, 35 or 38 from the Centraal side through the IJtunnel and get off at Hamerstraat, 191 m from the door, on the same OVpay tap.

Getting home after dark

  • 23:50

    Last F2 to IJplein

    Free ferry from behind Centraal, every six minutes, then nine minutes on foot. Aim for the 23:30 or 23:40 boat.

  • 24 hours

    F3 to Buiksloterweg

    Every four minutes. It lands 800 m west of the hotel, a 13 minute walk, and it runs all night.

  • N91, N93

    Night buses from Centraal

    Hourly into Noord until 07:30. N93 leaves Centraal at 01:14 and is in Molenwijk at 01:44. €5.70 for 90 minutes, tap a card, you cannot buy on board.

Fallback: a taxi or Uber from the centre to Noord is roughly €25 to €35 on the meter, an estimate rather than a published fare. Worth it once if the group splits.

Friday to Sunday

The times follow the ferry timetable and the 16:40 sunset.

Friday 20

9°, drizzle.

  1. Afternoon

    Train in, F2 ferry, dump the bags. No long walk on arrival.

  2. 17:00

    Oedipus taproom, five minutes away, for the first beer.

  3. 19:00

    Ferry over, then dinner at Bird Thai on the Zeedijk, walk in and veg sorted, or Bazar in De Pijp for the bigger room.

  4. 21:30

    In de Wildeman on Kolksteeg for the beer list, then Café Chris on Bloemstraat for a 1624 jenever.

  5. 23:30

    Aim for the 23:30 or 23:40 F2 from Centraal. Miss it and it is the F3 from Buiksloterweg, which never stops.

Saturday 21

10°, showers. Markets in the morning, Noord at midday, cocktails late.

  1. 09:30

    Noordermarkt and Lindengracht, Saturdays only, in the Jordaan. Breakfast from the stalls, F2 over.

  2. 12:00

    Ferry F4 to NDSM: Straat museum, street art and indoors, then a beer at Pllek. Both are under cover, which is why this half of Saturday sits at midday.

  3. 16:00

    Back across for sunset at A’DAM Lookout, swing optional, or a pint at Café de Ceuvel, 19 minutes on foot.

  4. 19:30

    Dinner at Wijmpje Beukers or Bazar in De Pijp, or Café-Restaurant Amsterdam for the big room with 33 vegetarian dishes.

  5. 22:00

    Door 74, same-day reservation from the morning on WhatsApp +31 6 34 04 51 22, 21 and over, groups to 12. Then Tales & Spirits, walk in until 01:00.

  6. Late

    Night bus N91 or N93 from Centraal, or the F3, which runs all night.

Sunday 22

6°, dry and clear, −2° overnight.

  1. 09:30

    Breakfast at the hotel, then ferry over.

  2. 10:30

    One small museum: Rembrandthuis, Ons’ Lieve Heer op Solder or Museum Van Loon.

  3. 12:30

    Wynand Fockink, Pijlsteeg 31, for a jenever. It opens early and takes twenty minutes.

  4. 13:30

    Last walk through the Jordaan. Shops mostly open around 12:00 on Sundays.

  5. 15:00

    F2 back, bags, then Centraal to Schiphol. Allow 45 minutes door to gate.

Dutch, and seven of you

Ten phrases to say out loud, and the practical side of moving as seven.

Moving as seven

The things that need deciding before Friday.

One taxi takes four. Restaurant tables for seven are the constraint, so ring the same afternoon even at walk-in places: “walk in” means no booking is required, not that a table for seven appears. Keep a shared pin on the hotel in Maps, and agree a meeting point before each night out. Café Chris in the Jordaan or In de Wildeman in the Centrum both work.

Book before you fly or drop it: Anne Frank House is online only with timed slots and goes days ahead, Van Gogh is timed and often sold out on the day. The Amsterdam Light Festival does not start until 26 November, six days after you leave, and the IJ-hallen flea market runs the first weekend of the month, so not this one.

The weather, and what to pack

Friday 9° with light drizzle, Saturday 10° and showery, Sunday 6° and dry with −2° overnight. Bring a coat and boots for wet cobbles, and check the forecast again the morning you fly.

Before you book a table

Ring ahead for anything on a Saturday after 20:00, even at walk-in places. The meat-led rooms are there for variety: the vegetarian eats sides and drinks beer, so do not make one the main-night dinner.

Beer in small glasses

Amsterdam beer comes in small glasses, a vaasje of 0.25 L, so you can work through a tap list. €5 to €7 a glass at a taproom, €7 to €9 at a specialist bar, €4 to €6 for a macro pilsner.
